Questions

Do I need a sleep tracker?

Stayful reads your sleep from Apple Health if you use a watch or a sleep app. Without one, it uses the usual bedtime and wake-up you enter, and you tap "I'm up" in the morning.

How accurate are the predictions?

They start from a model of sleep pressure, your body clock and caffeine. Each evening you can answer "Around what time did you crash?", and Insights show how close each prediction was. With Pro, predictions move to the time you actually crash.

Will a late coffee ruin my sleep?

Stayful tracks how much caffeine is still active and shows your last good time for a cup. When you log one after it, a notice tells you before you save. It also never suggests more than 400 mg of caffeine in a day, the level EFSA gives for adults.

Is Stayful a medical app?

No. Stayful gives estimates for personal planning only. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

How do I cancel Pro?

In your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

Is there an Android version?

Stayful is iPhone only for now.
