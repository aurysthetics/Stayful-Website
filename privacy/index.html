Privacy policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

The short version

  • Stayful has no account and no server of its own. Your sleep, coffees, naps and answers stay on your iPhone.
  • Your Apple Health data never leaves your iPhone.
  • If you say yes, Stayful sends anonymous usage stats and how far its crash predictions were off, so we can make them more accurate. If you say no, nothing is sent. You can change your mind at any time in Settings.
  • Purchases go through Apple. We use RevenueCat to know whether you have Pro, with an anonymous ID.
  • We never sell your data, never show ads and never track you across other apps.

This policy applies to the Stayful iOS app, its widgets and this website.

Data that stays on your iPhone

Stayful keeps what you enter on your device, so it can predict your energy through the day:

  • your sleep: bed and wake times you enter, or the sleep read from Apple Health;
  • your coffees, energy drinks and naps;
  • your answers to the evening crash question and the after-nap question;
  • your profile: body weight, caffeine sensitivity, usual sleep times and how long you usually take to fall asleep;
  • the busy times you enter, and your onboarding answers.

We can't see any of it. Your widgets read the same data from your iPhone. Reminders and alerts are scheduled on your iPhone as local notifications. Deleting the app deletes this data. Like any app's data, it can be part of your iPhone's own iCloud backup, which Apple handles.

Apple Health. If you allow it, Stayful reads Sleep only, never writes to Health and reads nothing else. Health data never leaves your iPhone and is never used for usage stats, advertising or anything other than your predictions. You can turn access off in the Health app or in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Health.

Usage stats and prediction accuracy (only if you agree)

When you set up Stayful, we ask whether you want to help make predictions more accurate. Nothing below is sent unless you say yes. You can turn it off at any time in Settings > Privacy > Share usage stats; from then on, nothing is sent and your anonymous ID is deleted.

What is sent

  • Usage events: which features you use, such as "coffee logged", "nap started", "Insights opened", "paywall shown" or which Pro plan you chose. Each event can only carry a value from a short fixed list (for example where a coffee was logged from: the app, a widget or a notification). It never carries an amount, a duration or a time.
  • Evening check-in: when you answer the evening crash question, whether you crashed, whether Stayful had predicted a crash that day, how many caffeinated drinks you had before the predicted crash and whether you napped before it.
  • Prediction accuracy: if you crashed, how far the predicted crash was from your answer, rounded to 15 minutes (for example "30 min early"), with a little context: how many caffeinated drinks and whether a nap came before your crash, the day of the week, and your caffeine sensitivity setting (low, normal or high).
  • Technical details sent automatically: app and iOS version, device model, language, region, time zone, hour of the day, accessibility settings, whether the app is a test version, and when the app is installed, updated, opened or closed.

Drink counts are only ever sent in broad groups (none, 1, 2, 3, 4 or more). Never the time of your crash, an amount in mg, a duration, or anything from Apple Health.

How it is sent. Through PostHog, an analytics service, on its EU servers (Frankfurt, Germany). Events are tied to a random ID created for your install, never to your name or Apple ID; it lets us see trends over time without knowing who you are. PostHog does not keep your IP address, and we turn off locating you from it. To keep volumes small, only a random share of installs may send events. Events are kept for up to 2 years, then deleted.

Why. To learn which features help, find what breaks, and tune the energy model so crash predictions get closer to real life. The legal basis is your consent.

Purchases

Stayful Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment, billing and taxes; we never see your name, email or card details.

To know whether you have Pro, Stayful uses RevenueCat. It receives an anonymous ID created by RevenueCat and your App Store purchase records (which plan, trial, renewal, restore). We send it nothing else: no profile, no attributes, no advertising data. RevenueCat is based in the United States; transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses. It keeps this data for as long as Stayful uses RevenueCat. The legal basis is providing the subscription you bought.

You manage or cancel your subscription in iOS Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions.

This website

stayful.app sets no cookies, has no analytics and loads nothing from other servers. It is hosted by GitHub Pages; like any web host, GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes (see the GitHub Privacy Statement).

What we never do

  • Sell or rent your data.
  • Show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.
  • Send anything from Apple Health off your iPhone.
  • Ask for your name, email, contacts, location or calendar.

Your choices and rights

  • Usage stats: on or off at any time in Settings > Privacy > Share usage stats.
  • Apple Health: on or off in the Health app.
  • Your data on the iPhone: delete it by deleting the app.
  • Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete data about you, object to its use, or withdraw your consent. Because we only hold anonymous IDs, we usually can't tell which data is yours; turning usage stats off deletes your ID and stops anything new from being sent. Write to us and we'll help as far as we can.
  • You can also complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr) or your local data protection authority.
