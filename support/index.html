Support

Questions, a bug, an idea? Write to contact@stayful.app. In the app, Settings › About › Send feedback opens an email to the same address.

Subscription

Pro doesn't show after buying, or on a new iPhone

Open the Pro screen and tap Restore. Use the same Apple ID you bought Pro with.

Cancel Pro

In your iPhone's Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions, then Stayful. Cancel at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Pro stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

Ask for a refund

Apple handles payments and refunds. Ask at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Using Stayful

My sleep from Apple Health doesn't show

In the Health app, tap your profile picture, then Apps, then Stayful, and turn on Sleep. Stayful reads Sleep only. Without Health data, it uses the usual bedtime and wake-up you set, and you tap "I'm up" in the morning.

Crash alerts or reminders don't come

In your iPhone's Settings, open Notifications › Stayful and allow notifications. With Pro, crash alerts also need to be on in Stayful's Settings.

Delete my data

Your sleep, coffees and naps live only on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes them. More in the privacy policy.
